## Reflection

_**Among the technical people in your life right now, who has the least power, least seniority, least confidence, least access, least context? What does that mean for how you show up as a leader for them?**_

I think my fellow members of the Curriculum Committee within Girls Who Code @ Columbia University have the least power, seniority, confidence, access, and context to the rest of GWC as a whole. They joined at the start of the spring semester and are new to the club, so they have limited context and access, since the expectation seems to be that they should integrate themselves as opposed to being led in the committee. We also have the least seniority and confidence in comparison to the executive board of GWC. I think I could show up better as a leader for them because I have more experience and knowledge of GWC and what it means to be in and do the work for the Curriculum Committee.

_**Who is this for? What was the unasked question? Why hadn't anyone answered it before?**_

I created a document for the Curriculum Committee within Girls Who Code @ Columbia University that serves as a central location for all the information and resources committee members need to complete their tasks successfully. The unasked question was "Why is all the information and tools we use consistently difficult to find and in multiple locations?". I don't think anyone answered it before since they resorted to finding the information or resource themselves, even if it took a long time, and got used to that process or even bookmarked what they needed for their own ease of access.
