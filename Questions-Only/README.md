# M4: Questions Only

*Help a friend debug a problem by only asking questions.*

**Target Skills: Constructive Inquiry, Verbal Precision, Accessible Communication**

## Task

The next time anyone asks for help debugging, resolve the issue using only questions. No direct answers. No keyboard-grabbing. No "have you tried..." suggestions in disguise.

## Reflection

_**Think about a time when someone asked you a question (in a class, on a team, in a workshop, etc) that made you feel stupid for not already knowing the answer. What was the question? What was the tone? Now think about a time when someone asked you a question that made you feel respected and curious, even though you didn't know the answer. What was different about how the second question was asked?**_

In my pre-calculus course in high school, I remember my teacher asking me a question that made me feel stupid for not already knowing the answer. The question pertained to something in our textbook, but he was not great at instruction or teaching, so I had no idea what the answer was. I do remember the tone was very harsh and aggressive. In contrast, my computer science teachers in high school always asked questions that made me feel respected and curious, even if I had no idea what the answers were, because they were kind and supportive in tone.
